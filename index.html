How it works

🎯

The most useful words first

These are the words you'll really need to manage a basic conversation. Learn them and the rest builds on top.

🃏

Sessions feel different each time

The app rotates between three styles: tap to reveal, type the answer, or pick from four. Every few cards, a quick matching round mixes things up. It picks for you — just tap "Study now."

✅

Be honest after each card

Not sure: you didn't know it.
Recognize: you knew it once you saw the answer.
Know it: instant, no hesitation.

If you really blank on a typing card, tap "I don't know — show me" to reveal the answer without guessing. Honest taps keep the app accurate.

⏭️

Already past the basics?

On any new card you'll see an "I already know this" button. Tap it to skip the word and mark it mastered, so the app meets you at your level.

📊

Watch your progress

Your home screen shows the word of the day, the last 30 days as dots, and progress bars per theme. Cross a milestone (10, 25, 50… mastered) and you'll get a little celebration.

🏆

A little, often

A word only counts as "mastered" once you've genuinely remembered it. You can't rush it, and you don't need to. A few minutes a day is all it takes.
